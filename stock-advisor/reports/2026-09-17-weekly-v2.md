### Druckenmiller — top-down, 12–18 months out

His rule is to position for where liquidity and the cycle will be in 12–18 months, not where they are. Written from the tables in this report:

- **What has already happened:** the Fed's first hike since 2023 into an oil shock. Energy (+16% RS over 3 months), refiners at highs, insurers and health care absorbing money, rate-sensitive groups sold: utilities −24% RS over 6 months, homebuilders −20% from their high, real estate and small caps under their 50-day. That trade is crowded now; the CANSLIM list below is full of it.
- **What a hiking Fed plus $100 oil usually produces next** is a growth scare within two to four quarters, and then the Fed stops. The groups that are washed out today (utilities, homebuilders, REITs, staples, small caps, long Treasuries at −9% from high) are where that rotation goes when it comes. None of them is turning yet by the table's own test. The instruction is to build the list and wait for the turn, not to buy the bottom.
- **The one cluster that is both cheap and turning** is software and IT services: Adobe, Autodesk, Salesforce, Workday, Accenture, ServiceNow, Gartner, Cognizant, GoDaddy, Oracle. All are 25–55% off their highs on the "AI replaces software" narrative, most trade at 9–15× forward earnings with 20–60% returns on equity, and several have just reclaimed their 50-day with positive one-month relative strength. That is the Buffett list and the rotation list pointing at the same names, which is the setup this report exists to find. The bear case is real and has to be read in each 10-K, but the prices already assume it.
- **Semis** led over six months and are now breaking down relative to the market (−12% over 3 months). Late-cycle behavior for the AI-capex trade; the model says wait for a base, not buy the dip.
- **China internet** (your BABA) is the most washed-out theme in the book at −40% from its high and −33% relative strength over six months, and it is **not yet turning**: below the 50-day, one-month relative strength −7.6%. It is exactly the kind of position this section is for, but the trigger has not fired. The turn signal is KWEB above its 50-day with positive one-month relative strength, and for BABA specifically a reclaim of the 50-day at $118.
- **Bitcoin is the odd one out:** turning hard (+21% relative strength in a month) against a hiking Fed. Either liquidity is coming from somewhere the Fed doesn't control, or it is a crowded reflex. Your MSTR and ASST exposure already expresses this view; the rules say don't add to it.
