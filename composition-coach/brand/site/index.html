How it works

Point. Tap. Look.

  1. Point and follow the cue.

    One small sign shows where to move, with a word or two: Slightly right

  2. Tap once.

    The ring around the shutter turns green when it's right, and the photo takes itself. Hold to shoot right away.

  3. See why it works.

    A short card shows what worked and one thing to try next time. That's the whole lesson.
