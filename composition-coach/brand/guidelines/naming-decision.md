# Naming decision — SceneRight

Approved by the user on October 19, 2026.

Product name: **SceneRight**. Lowercase logo: **sceneright**. Small logo descriptor: **photo**.

Tagline: **The photo you meant to take.**

Support line: **Frames for you. Shows you why.**

The name connects the scene with the intended photograph and sounds like “seen right.” It suits a calm camera that offers small physical framing cues. The main verbal ambiguity is SceneRight / SeenRight; use the written SceneRight form consistently.

## Why the earlier option was retired

The previously explored name justSo Photo was retired after verifying an existing Just So Camera listing on Google Play. Its stated purpose includes teaching composition while shooting, which creates a close product-category overlap. Adding “photo” did not resolve that naming concern.

Verified source: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.studiowj.cameraApp

Eyebright, SecondEye, ReFrame, JustRight, and other suggestions were considered during the discussion. They are not the approved product name.

## Availability status

Preliminary indexed-web searches for SceneRight and scene right, including Apple and Google storefront domains, did not reveal an obvious exact-name camera-app match. That is limited negative search evidence, not a guarantee of availability or trademark clearance. No registration, legal search, or domain purchase was performed.

## Implementation scope

This package updates the brand assets and landing page. It supplies Android icon resource assets, but no actual Android app project was supplied. App labels, running-app strings, release notes, and functional status-color implementations must be updated in the real app repository once that project is available. Do not change an Android application identifier solely for a marketing rename.

The warm marketing palette is approved. Retain the existing in-app green ready and amber warning colors over dark neutral live-camera surfaces; their exact values are not specified here.
